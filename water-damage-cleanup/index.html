Water damage cleanup is what happens after the water is stopped: removing wet debris, sanitizing surfaces and deodorizing so the home is safe to dry and rebuild. Call (949) 670-0939 if you have a flooded room, a failed appliance or contaminated water in Lake Forest, CA, and keep children and pets out of the affected area until a crew has assessed it.

Cleanup is time sensitive because wet carpet pad, insulation and drywall can go from salvageable to a mold risk in a day or two. The first 48 hours decide how much has to be thrown away and how much can be saved.

The first 48 hours after water damage

In the first two days, the work is sorting. A crew separates what can dry in place from what cannot, bags and removes the rest, and cleans hard surfaces before drying equipment goes in. Skipping that sorting step and running fans over contaminated materials only moves bacteria around.

Our dispatch line runs 24/7, so cleanup calls on a weekend or at night are normal. Same-day response is offered when crews are available, and the dispatcher will give you straight answers on what can wait until morning.

Photograph first

Take photos of damaged items and rooms before anything is moved. Insurance claims rely on them.

Do not use household vacuums

A regular vacuum is not built for water and can be a shock hazard. Wait for extraction equipment.

Keep wet items separate

Move wet rugs and boxes to a hard surface outdoors if it is safe, and leave soaked drywall and insulation alone.

Clean water, gray water and black water

Industry practice sorts water into three categories. Category 1 comes from a clean source such as a supply line. Category 2 has significant contamination, such as washing machine discharge. Category 3 is grossly contaminated, such as sewage or rising floodwater.

Category matters because it decides which materials stay. Carpet and pad that took Category 1 water can sometimes be dried, while the same materials in Category 3 water are removed. For sewage or other contaminated water, the EPA tells homeowners to call a professional who has experience cleaning buildings damaged by contaminated water.

Sewage backup cleanup

A sewage backup needs extra caution. Stay out of the water, do not run water down drains, and shut off power to the affected area only if you can reach the panel dry and safely. Call 911 or your utility for any electrical or gas concern before you call us.

Crews extract the water, remove contaminated porous materials, clean and disinfect hard surfaces and then dry the structure.
