Flood damage restoration deals with water that comes from outside or from the ground up: storm runoff, creek overflow, drainage failure or a sewer line that reverses. In Lake Forest, CA, that means pumping out the building, removing contaminated materials and drying what is left. Call (949) 670-0939 any time.

Floodwater is treated as Category 3, so the first rule is to stay out of it. Do not walk through standing water, do not touch electrical equipment in a wet room, and do not wait for the water to recede on its own.

Flooding emergencies in Lake Forest

Flooding needs the fastest response because contaminated water contacts more materials every hour. Our dispatch line runs 24/7, and same-day response is offered when crews are available. Call before the rain stops if water is entering the building.

Orange County's hazard plan records a 1997 storm that severely damaged Lake Forest and lists Serrano Creek and Borrego among areas flooded in severe storms. The National Weather Service recorded 3 to 6 inches of rain on Orange County foothills in the atmospheric river of January 9 and 10, 2023, and lowlands saw 0.5 to 2 inches. These events are why local storms can overwhelm drainage even in a dry climate.

Stay out of the water

Floodwater can carry sewage and chemicals. Wait for a crew before wading through it.

Mind the power

If water reached outlets or the panel, call your utility or 911 before touching anything electrical.

Document everything

Photograph the high-water line and damaged items. Flood claims depend on documentation.

Basement flooding cleanup

If your home has a basement, lower level or sunken room, flooding there is a dewatering job first. Crews pump water out at a controlled rate so saturated soil and foundation walls do not shift, then remove wet drywall, carpet and insulation, and dry the space.

Restoration is not waterproofing. If water keeps entering after a storm, a drainage or waterproofing contractor addresses the cause, and we restore what was damaged.

Sewage backup cleanup after flooding

Heavy rain can overload sewer lines and push sewage back into drains. That water is Category 3, so porous materials come out, hard surfaces are cleaned and disinfected, and the structure is dried. The EPA says to call a professional with experience in contaminated water for sewage cleanup.

If the backup comes from the main line, ask your sewer agency to check it. In Lake Forest that is Irvine Ranch Water District in most areas and El Toro Water District in the southeast, so confirm which one is on your bill.
