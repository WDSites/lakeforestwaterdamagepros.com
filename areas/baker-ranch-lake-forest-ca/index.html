What Baker Ranch is

Baker Ranch is a master-planned community in the northwest part of Lake Forest, between Bake Parkway and Alton Parkway. A 2014 builder release says residents enter from Bake Parkway at Dimension Drive. It was developed by Shea Homes and Toll Brothers in partnership.

The grand opening of the first six neighborhoods was February 8, 2014, and the project was announced as a 2,380-home community. That is the planned total from the release, and we have not verified how many homes stand today.

The city's guide map shows Baker Ranch Community Park, and the Foothill Ranch Towne Center sits along the community's northern side according to a local guide. The community also backs onto open space, which the builders described as about 900 acres in 2014.

Why newer homes call about water

A home built after 2014 is not likely to have old original pipes, but new houses have their own pressure points. Washing machine hoses, dishwasher lines, refrigerator ice-maker lines, water heaters and air conditioner drain lines are all installed in a new home and all can fail.

Baker Ranch includes townhomes and multi-story houses, so water from an upstairs fixture can travel through a ceiling and down a wall before anyone notices. We do not claim these problems are common here. We only describe where the water goes when they happen.

Warranty, association and documentation

If your home is recent, ask whether a builder warranty or association rule affects who fixes what. Do that in parallel with drying, not before it, because wet drywall and flooring cannot wait for paperwork.

Photos, moisture readings and a written scope help explain what was damaged and what was done. Your insurer decides coverage, so ask what it needs to see and keep every receipt.

How drying works

After extraction, crews map moisture in walls and floors, set air movers and dehumidifiers and return to confirm readings. Our process follows the published ANSI/IICRC S500 reference, and we explain what must be removed and what can be dried before work begins.
