What Lake 1 is

Lake 1 is the name used for the Lake Forest Beach and Tennis Club, the older of the city's two lake communities. A 2018 Patch report said the club turned 50 on August 14, 2018, which puts its opening around 1968. Its clubhouse is on Ridge Route.

The same report said association dues vary by tract, from about ten dollars a month when the club began to as much as 250 dollars a month now. That tells you Lake 1 contains several tracts under one association.

Lakeside upkeep and your responsibility

The Patch report described association spending on the lake's quay wall and a rebuilt lagoon, and a replacement of more than 500,000 square feet of turf with drought-tolerant plants using water district rebates. Those are association projects, separate from the water inside your own home.

That distinction helps when something goes wrong. Lake-edge and common-area water is for the association to assess. Water that comes from your plumbing, appliance or roof is the work we are asked to dry.

Established homes and what we check first

A community that has existed since the late 1960s has had plenty of remodels, so we do not guess what is inside a wall. We measure moisture with meters, open only what the readings point to and explain the scope before work starts.

Homes built before 1978 may need lead-safe work practices, and some older ceiling coatings may contain asbestos. We tell you when a test should happen before any demolition.

Drying follows the published ANSI/IICRC S500 reference, and readings are repeated on later days to confirm the structure is dry.
