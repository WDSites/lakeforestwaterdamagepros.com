Rancho Santa Margarita sits east of Mission Viejo, and our 24/7 emergency line at (949) 670-0939 covers it from Lake Forest. We serve ZIP code 92688 with extraction, drying and rebuild.

RSM incorporated on January 1, 2000 as Orange County's 33rd city, and it has about 45,779 residents in 12.9 square miles. Its water story is shaped by canyons, creeks and one water district.

One water district, many canyons

Santa Margarita Water District serves Rancho Santa Margarita and Mission Viejo, plus Coto de Caza, Las Flores, Ladera Ranch, Rancho Mission Viejo and Talega. The district covers about 111 square miles and was formed in 1964.

If a main fails in your street, SMWD is the agency to call, and we handle the damage inside. The district also supplies recycled water, about 3 billion gallons a year, mainly for irrigation.

Flash floods in Trabuco Creek and Tijeras Canyon

The city's draft safety element says flood risk is mainly along Arroyo Trabuco, also called Trabuco Creek, and Tijeras Canyon Creek. Flows are short and high-peak, and dense brush in the channel may raise flood levels.

FEMA 100-year and 500-year zones exist in the city, but the document says no homes or structures are in them because they lie in open space. Dove Canyon is cited as a runoff area, so owners there should keep drains clear.

When water is spreading in Rancho Santa Margarita

Water is an emergency when it moves, when it contacts power or when it came from outside. Our line is open 24/7, and same-day response is offered when crews are available. Call before you start mopping.

Communities and lake access

The city includes the Rancho Santa Margarita Planned Community, Robinson Ranch, Dove Canyon, Rancho Cielo, Trabuco Highlands and Walden. First homes in the planned community were sold in 1986, so most housing is a few decades old.

Lake Rancho Santa Margarita has a walking path maintained by SAMLARC. Santa Margarita Parkway runs through the city and is the usual landmark for directions.

Permits and insurance

The Building and Safety Division is at 22112 El Paseo and its phone is 949-635-1800. Counter hours are Monday to Thursday and Friday morning. Permit rules vary by job, so ask the city building department before demolition.

Homeowners policies generally do not cover flood damage but do cover other kinds of water damage, according to the California Department of Insurance. Keep photos and receipts.
