How we pick the details on each page

A leak in an older Mission Viejo house is not the same problem as flash flooding along Trabuco Creek or a storm-season roof leak in Aliso Viejo.

Each city page uses that city's own records: who supplies the water, which agency issues permits, which creeks and channels carry storm runoff and how old the housing is. We use only facts we could confirm from city, county, state and federal sources, and where a fact was not available we say to check with the city or the FEMA flood map instead.
