Villages, washes and where flooding concentrates

Irvine incorporated on December 28, 1971 and grew village by village. The city's history page lists Turtle Rock, University Park, Culverdale, the Ranch and Walnut as completed by 1970, so some Irvine homes are older than many people assume.

The city's draft safety element places FEMA 100-year and 500-year flood zones in pockets, mostly along San Diego Creek, Serrano Creek, Borrego Canyon Wash, Agua Chinon Wash, Bee Canyon Wash, Peters Canyon Wash and Sand Canyon Wash. If you live near one of these, ask your insurer about flood coverage and check your address on the FEMA flood map.
