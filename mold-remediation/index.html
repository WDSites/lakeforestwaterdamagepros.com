I see spots on my wall or ceiling

Most mold calls begin with a spot. People notice dark patches in a closet corner, a ceiling near a bathroom or a wall behind furniture, and they wonder whether it is mold and whether it is dangerous. Color does not tell you what it is, and health questions belong to a physician.

What you can do is find the moisture. A recent plumbing leak, a roof leak, an AC condensate line or a bathroom fan that does not vent outside usually explains it. Fix the source, then call us to scope the removal. The process below is water mitigation for the moisture and remediation for the growth.

How a mold remediation job is set up

1

Moisture readings locate the leak or humidity problem.

Affected porous materials come out and surfaces are cleaned with HEPA vacuums.

4

Dry and verify

The area is dried and moisture is confirmed before rebuild.

Containment keeps disturbed mold from drifting into clean rooms. Air scrubbers with HEPA filters run during work, and workers wear respirators and protective suits. Porous materials such as drywall and insulation with growth are removed and bagged, while hard surfaces are cleaned.

We do not promise that mold will never return. What we do is fix the conditions that allowed it and confirm the area is dry before anything is closed up.

Mold removal and black mold cleanup

Black mold is a color, not a diagnosis. Many species look dark, and a lab is the only way to name one. California's health department advises against testing visible mold and against using bleach, so the practical decision is whether the area is small enough to clean or large enough to need a professional.

The EPA says that if mold covers less than about 10 square feet, you can in most cases handle it yourself. If it covers more, or if it is inside walls, above ceilings or came with sewage or flood, call a professional.

Mold after water damage

Mold after water damage is mostly a timing problem. Wet drywall, insulation and carpet pad that stay damp longer than about a day or two are the likeliest places for growth, and it can hide inside wall cavities for weeks before a smell or stain shows it.

That is why fast emergency water removal and drying prevent most mold calls, and why we recommend checking for hidden moisture when a leak went unnoticed. If mold has formed, remediation removes it and drying finishes the job.

Clearance testing and real estate deals

Some callers need proof that mold is gone, for example because a buyer's lender will not fund a loan until the issue is resolved. Independent clearance testing is done by a third-party assessor so the company that did the work is not grading its own job. We do not perform that testing, and we can coordinate with an assessor you hire.

California has no state license for mold assessors or remediators, so ask any company whether it works to S520, what the written scope covers and who inspects afterward.
